Before work begins, both parties will agree in writing on the project scope, deliverables, features, timeline, and price. Written confirmation may be provided through WhatsApp, email, or a project proposal. Requests outside the agreed scope will be treated as additional work and quoted separately.

3. Payment Terms

An advance payment of 30% of the agreed project price is required before work begins. The remaining 70% is due upon delivery. Payments are accepted through bank transfer, JazzCash, and EasyPaisa. If a payment becomes overdue, CodVion may pause work until the outstanding amount is received. A late payment fee of 2% of the overdue amount per week may apply. Project deadlines will be extended by the duration of the payment delay.

5. Timeline and Delays

CodVion commits to the project deadline agreed upon by both parties. If a delay is caused by CodVion, the Client will receive a discount of 2% of the project price for each full week of delay, up to a maximum discount of 10%. Delays caused by the Client, including late content, delayed feedback, overdue payments, or changes to requirements, will extend the project deadline and will not qualify for a discount.

The Client must provide the content, images, logos, access credentials, information, and approvals required for the project on time. The Client is responsible for ensuring that all supplied materials are legally owned or appropriately licensed for their intended use. CodVion is not responsible for copyright claims or other legal issues arising from materials supplied by the Client.

7. Revisions

Each project includes revisions within the agreed scope. Requests that exceed the agreed scope, introduce new features, or change previously agreed requirements will be treated as additional work and charged separately.

8. Delivery, Review and Acceptance

The Client has 7 days from delivery to review the work and report any issues. If no issues are reported within this period, the delivered work will be considered accepted. Preview or staging access may be provided during development. Such access does not transfer ownership of the work to the Client.

9. Free Support

CodVion provides 7 days of free support from delivery to address bugs in the delivered work. This support does not include new features, content changes, failures involving third party services, or issues caused by changes made by anyone other than CodVion.

10. Paid Maintenance

After the free support period ends, ongoing maintenance, updates, and technical support are available through paid maintenance plans. The scope, fees, and terms of any maintenance plan will be agreed upon separately.

Final source code, administrator access, and project credentials will be handed over after full payment has been received. CodVion retains ownership of its preexisting tools, libraries, frameworks, and reusable components. The Client is granted the right to use these components as part of the delivered project.

12. Third Party Costs

Costs associated with domains, hosting, paid plugins, APIs, licenses, and other third party services will be determined for each project and stated in the proposal or project agreement.

SEO and marketing results depend on factors outside CodVion’s control, including search engine algorithms, competition, and platform policies. CodVion may provide estimated results and timelines based on experience. However, specific rankings, traffic levels, follower counts, leads, or sales are not guaranteed.

14. Embedded Systems, IoT and AI Projects

The Client is responsible for purchasing any required hardware, sensors, boards, and devices unless otherwise agreed in writing. Hardware warranties are provided by the relevant manufacturer or seller. AI and machine learning systems are trained and tested using the available data. Their accuracy and performance may vary under real world conditions. CodVion does not guarantee a specific accuracy level unless expressly stated in the project agreement.

15. Cancellation and Refunds

If the Client cancels a project, the value of the work already completed will be deducted from the advance payment. Any remaining balance from that advance will be refunded. If the value of the completed work exceeds the advance payment, the Client must pay the outstanding difference. Refunds are not available for work that has already been delivered and accepted.

16. Termination

Either party may terminate the project agreement by providing 7 days of written notice. CodVion may suspend or terminate the agreement immediately if the Client seriously breaches these Terms, including through nonpayment, illegal use of the services, or abusive behavior. Upon termination, the Client remains responsible for payment for all work completed up to the termination date.

CodVion Internship Program Terms

Last updated: October 27, 2026 These Terms of Service (“Terms”) apply to the CodVion Internship Program available at intern.codvion.org (“Program”), operated by CodVion (“CodVion”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). By applying to or participating in the Program, you (“Intern”, “you”, or “your”) agree to these Terms.

1. About the Program

The CodVion Internship Program is a free, remote learning program in which participants develop practical skills through assigned tasks and a final project. The Program offers different tracks. The duration of each internship depends on the selected track and is stated in the relevant track details.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must have the basic skills required for their selected track. Applicants must be at least 16 years old. Applicants younger than 18 should obtain permission from a parent or guardian before applying. All information provided in the application must be accurate and truthful. False or misleading information may result in rejection or removal from the Program.

3. Application and Selection

Applications are reviewed based on the information provided, the applicant’s CV, and relevant skills. Submitting an application does not guarantee admission. CodVion may accept or reject applications and may limit the number of participants in a particular track or batch.

4. Free Participation, No Payment and No Job Guarantee

The Program is free to join. Interns are not required to pay a participation fee and do not receive a salary or stipend. Participation does not create an employment relationship or guarantee a job, paid position, or future work with CodVion. Strong performers may be considered for future opportunities, but such opportunities are not guaranteed.

5. Format and Time Commitment

The internship is conducted entirely online. The time commitment is flexible and based on assigned tasks and deadlines rather than fixed weekly working hours. Interns are expected to complete their work on time and participate in the Program’s communication channels.

6. Communication

Program announcements, tasks, guidance, and support are shared through WhatsApp groups, email, and Google Meet sessions. Interns must check these channels regularly, communicate respectfully, and keep their contact information accurate and up to date.

7. Tasks and Final Project

Interns work on training and practice assignments only. They do not work on real client projects. Each intern must complete the assigned tasks and submit a final project within the duration of their selected track. Late submissions may affect task marks and the overall performance evaluation.

8. Use of AI Tools

Interns may use AI tools, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot, to assist with tasks and the final project. However, interns remain responsible for their submissions. They must understand their work and be able to explain every part of it. Work that an intern cannot explain may receive lower marks or be rejected.

9. Originality and Honest Work

All submissions must reflect the intern’s own effort and understanding, including when permitted AI tools are used. Copying another person’s work, submitting work that you do not understand, or presenting someone else’s work as your own is not allowed.

10. Certificate

To qualify for the CodVion Internship Certificate, interns must complete the full duration of their track, submit all assigned tasks, and complete and submit the final project. Overall performance may also be considered when determining certificate eligibility. Certificates are issued as a PDF or image and include a unique Certificate ID that can be used for online verification. CodVion may revoke a certificate if plagiarism, copied work, false information, or misconduct is discovered, including after the certificate has been issued. A revoked certificate will no longer appear as valid through online verification.

11. Code of Conduct

Interns must treat CodVion team members and other participants respectfully and professionally. Harassment, abusive behavior, spam, cheating, inappropriate content, and misuse of the Program’s communication channels are prohibited.

12. Removal from the Program

CodVion may remove an intern for inactivity, repeatedly missed deadlines, breaches of these Terms, or misconduct. An intern who is removed from the Program will not receive a certificate.

13. Leaving the Program

Interns may leave the Program at any time without providing advance notice. Interns who leave before completing the Program requirements will not receive a certificate. If you decide to leave, please inform CodVion through the Program’s WhatsApp group or by email.

14. Use of Your Work

CodVion may display and use tasks and final projects completed during the Program, including on its website, in its portfolio, and on social media, without obtaining separate permission or providing credit to the intern. Interns retain the right to include their own work in their personal portfolios and CVs.

15. Confidentiality

Interns must keep confidential any information about CodVion, its team, internal processes, or clients that is not publicly available and that they learn during the Program. Such information must not be shared with others. CodVion will handle interns’ personal information as described in its Privacy Policy.

16. Privacy and Data

When you apply, CodVion collects information such as your name, email address, phone number, city, country, education, skills, CV, and any links you provide. Our website uses analytics and tracking tools, including Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. Please read our Privacy Policy for details about how personal information is collected and used.

17. Disclaimer and Liability

The Program is provided for learning and practice. CodVion does not guarantee any particular learning result, employment opportunity, income, or career outcome. To the extent permitted by law, CodVion is not liable for loss or damage arising from participation in the Program.

18. Changes to the Program and Terms

CodVion may change, pause, or discontinue the Program or any of its tracks at any time. CodVion may also update these Terms. Updated Terms take effect from the date displayed at the top of this page.

19. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan. If a dispute arises, CodVion and the intern will first discuss the matter in good faith and seek a resolution.

20. Contact
